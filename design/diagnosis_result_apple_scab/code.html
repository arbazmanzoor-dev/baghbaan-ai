What is Apple Scab?

Pathogen cycle & weather risk

expand_more

Apple Scab is caused by the fungal pathogen Venturia inaequalis. Overwintering spores in fallen orchard floor leaves discharge during mild, rain-soaked spring days.

water_drop Risk spikes when leaves stay wet for over 9 continuous hours at 15°C to 24°C.

C

Immediate Action & Prevention

Step-by-step orchard containment

expand_more
1
Sanitation & Pruning

Rake and burn or deeply compost all fallen orchard floor leaves. Prune dense canopies to promote sunlight and wind circulation.

2
Foliar Urea Application

Spray 5% agricultural urea solution onto leaf litter prior to snowfall to accelerate microbial breakdown of pseudothecia.

3
Recommended Chemical / Bio Protocol

Apply protective protectant fungicides (Mancozeb 75 WP or Captan 50 WP) prior to predicted spring wet spells, or curative DMI (Difenoconazole 25 EC) strictly within 72h of infection period.
