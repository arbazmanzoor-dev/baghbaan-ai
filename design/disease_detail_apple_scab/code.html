Venturia inaequalis • Ascomycete

خلاصہ برائے باغبان • Orchard Alert

Most devastating fungal epidemic in the Kashmir valley. Directly attacks young leaves and developing calyx during wet spring (March–May), causing premature defoliation and severe unmarketable russeted scabs.

yard Visual Progression by Season

3 Stages
Mar-Apr
Primary Stage

Spring Foliage Outbreak

Velvety olive-brown circular spots on the upper leaf blade with slight curling, blistering, and puckering along leaf margins.

May-Jul
Secondary Infection

Summer Necrosis & Drop

Dark, thickened, and hardened raised crusts. Foliage chlorosis (yellowing) causes catastrophic early defoliation, starving fruit buds.

Jul-Aug
Grade Loss: 80%

Fruit Russet & Distortion

Corky, fissured black scabs. Developing fruit becomes misshapen, cracks open, and allows secondary secondary rot pathogens to enter.

vaccines SKUAST-K Spray Schedule

Official SOP

Strict prophylactic timeline endorsed by Sher-e-Kashmir University of Agricultural Sciences.

1
Silver Tip / Green Tip Stage I

Timing: Early March

Mancozeb 75 WP (0.3%) or Captan 50 WP (0.3%)

Creates protective surface barrier across nascent bud scales.

2
Pink Bud Stage Crucial Window

Timing: Prior to Blossom Opening

Dodine 65 WP (0.075%) or Difenoconazole 25 EC (0.03%)

Curative translaminar penetration to knock out spore germination.

3
Petal Fall (Fruit Set) Stage III

Timing: 70–80% Petal Drop

Systemic Fungicide (Myclobutanil 10 WP)

Apply strictly within 48 hours of rain spells to prevent calyx infection.

compost Sanitation & Non-Chemical Measures

shower

Autumn 5% Urea Spray

Spray 50g urea per liter of water on foliage just before leaf fall or onto fallen leaf litter. Accelerates microbial breakdown of leaf matter, destroying 85%+ overwintering fungal ascospores before snowfall.

content_cut

Canopy Aeration & Thinning

Annual pruning to ensure maximum air flow and direct sunlight through inner scaffolding branches, keeping leaf wetness time below the 9-hour critical threshold.

policy

Field Warning & Resistance Prevention

Avoid consecutive sprays of single-site systemic fungicides (e.g., Strobilurins or DMI). Repeated usage triggers resistant scab strains across the orchard district.
